Where the meanings collide

  • One emoji, several meanings. ⚡ is Quick Add (×3), a training day (×4) and “analysing” (×1). 📊 is History, an empty state and the bar-chart toggle. 💧 is a badge, a callout and a data marker. ⚖ is a callout, a series toggle, a header and a data marker.
  • One meaning, several emoji. Done: ✓ ✅ 👍. Edit: ✎ ✏. Remove: ✕ × 🗑. Warning: ⚠ 🛡 ⛽ 🔄. AI: 🤖 ✨ 🏋 🧑‍💼. Premium or locked: ⭐ 🔐 🔏 🔓. Body measurement: 📏 📐.

Icon

Only for a tap target or a status that repeats across screens. One meaning, one glyph.

Word

When the button already has a label, or the emoji only stood in for a word (CSV, Report, Sign out, Line / Bar).

State

Training or rest, badge tier, chart series, recording, learning. Show these with colour, a dot or a pill.

Delete

Decoration in copy, hero glyphs above headlines, confetti, empty-state art, neutral callouts.
