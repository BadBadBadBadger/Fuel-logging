  • Day type is stored apart from the chosen mode. A 2,841 day is saved to History as Maintain, but the mode setting stays Cut. Tomorrow opens in Cut with the CUT lamp lit, and today keeps looking like a cut day. Writing the effective type back into the mode is the v99 bug class, where one big day silently switched the user to Maintain.
  • A maintenance-level day counts as rest. A day saved as Maintain or Bulk drains cut load like a banked break day (1/14). It does not accrue load, so a blow-out day is not punished twice.
  • No over-zone. The gauge is drawn exactly as in a2: same arc, same 11 ticks, E and F in the same place. At or over target the arc is simply full with the pointer at F. Only the colour and the "N over" readout show how far over the day went.
  • Maintain and Bulk are neutral. There is no green, amber or red in those modes, under or over. The readout still says "still to go" or "N over". Colour exists only to protect a cut. The green is a muted sage (#8cc47a) and the red is #e8604c, a clearer red than the pink-brick Delete (#c9836f).
