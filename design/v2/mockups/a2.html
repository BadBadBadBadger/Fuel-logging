What changed in v2

  • The tank fills as you eat. The amber arc and pointer now show eaten as a share of target (1,150 / 2,241, 51%) moving from E toward F. The readout is the big 1,091 with a small "kcal" on the same baseline, labelled "still to go".
  • No glow anywhere. All text-shadow and glow box-shadows are gone from the readout, the mode lamps and the phone frame. Amber is flat.
  • Two figures, no sum. The target − eaten = left sum is replaced by TARGET 2,241 and EATEN 1,150, centred under the gauge. Target has a dashed underline: tap it to type your own figure, as in the current app (spec targets/02), with no pencil. The panel below the phone shows the edit state.
  • Macro hint removed. The tapes say it already, so the protein/fat sentence and its space are gone.
  • Actions moved up, in flow. LOG FOOD (amber, photo / voice / text, AI) and QUICK ADD (amber outline) sit straight under the macros and above the meals, instead of in a bottom bar.
  • Meal list replaces the docket. Rows with the time in a left gutter, name, quiet macros below, kcal on the right and hairlines between, as in Direction C. There are no per-row buttons: tapping a row opens it in place. Chicken and rice is shown open with name, kcal and P/C/F fields, plus Save, Save to Quick Add and a quiet Delete. Below the actions sits "Re-estimate from name" (Pro), with no AI label because the estimate is understood to be AI. It re-reads the name as typed, refills kcal and P/C/F, and shows "Re-estimating…" and then "Updated".
  • Measurements always have a way in. A permanent, quiet row under weight and TDEE shows the last neck/waist (hip too for women) with when they were logged and a Log action. When the weekly check is due it gets a small amber dot and DUE, not a pop-up "Log now / Not now" nag. It stays on Today, undoing v1's move to History. The 40 / 95 cm figures are placeholders.
  • Workout gets its own compact row next to water and weight (Legs, 45 min, moderate, 486 kcal, with Log workout and paste log for pro).
  • Unchanged: header, mode lamps, tapes, water strip, weight and TDEE, the 7-day lamps, zero emoji, and the same six hand-drawn icons (History, Badges, Settings, photo, voice, text) on one 24px grid with a 1.75px stroke.
  • Signed-in chip. The Google profile chip is the last item in the header, after History, Badges and Settings. It is a 36px circle so it reads as a person, not a tool. It shows the photo, or the fallback shown here: the initial "D" (the dev-preview user) in the accent colour with a thin amber ring. It opens Account & Privacy. To fit at 390px the header buttons went from 38 to 36px with 5px gaps, and the date line is 11px.
