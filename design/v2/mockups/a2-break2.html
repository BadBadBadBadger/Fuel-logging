  • The 90% line. A break day is banked only if intake reaches 90% of maintenance: 2,741 × 0.9 ≈ 2,470 kcal. Going over is fine. The counter shows banked days, not calendar days, and each banked day drains 1/14 of the cut load.
  • Judged after the day ends. Today is always "in progress" (W3). Yesterday is decided the next morning. Under the line, the counter holds and the tape gets a hatched gap: neutral grey for one day (W4), amber once there are two or more (W5).
  • Unlogged is not banked. A day with no food is not banked, but it is shown as pending (a dashed gap) rather than failed, with a "Log it" action (W6, W7). Logging it later can still bank it. The gauge is unchanged in every state.
