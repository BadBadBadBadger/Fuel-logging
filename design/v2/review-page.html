Why Fuel Log looks generated, and three ways out

Five agents looked at the shipped Today screen. One diagnosed it against the code, three each redesigned it in a different direction, and one audited every emoji. All three directions use the same data as the screenshot below.

Mockups only. Nothing in the app's code was changed.

What changed in v2

  • The tank fills as you eat. The amber arc and pointer now show eaten as a share of target (1,150 / 2,241, 51%) moving from E toward F. The readout is the big 1,091 with a small "kcal" on the same baseline, labelled "still to go".
  • No glow anywhere. All text-shadow and glow box-shadows are gone from the readout, the mode lamps and the phone frame. Amber is flat.
  • Two figures, no sum. The target − eaten = left sum is replaced by TARGET 2,241 and EATEN 1,150, centred under the gauge. Target has a dashed underline: tap it to type your own figure, as in the current app (spec targets/02), with no pencil. The panel below the phone shows the edit state.
  • Macro hint removed. The tapes say it already, so the protein/fat sentence and its space are gone.
  • Actions moved up, in flow. LOG FOOD (amber, photo / voice / text, AI) and QUICK ADD (amber outline) sit straight under the macros and above the meals, instead of in a bottom bar.
  • Meal list replaces the docket. Rows with the time in a left gutter, name, quiet macros below, kcal on the right and hairlines between, as in Direction C. There are no per-row buttons: tapping a row opens it in place. Chicken and rice is shown open with name, kcal and P/C/F fields, plus Save, Save to Quick Add and a quiet Delete. Below the actions sits "Re-estimate from name" (Pro), with no AI label because the estimate is understood to be AI. It re-reads the name as typed, refills kcal and P/C/F, and shows "Re-estimating…" and then "Updated".
  • Measurements always have a way in. A permanent, quiet row under weight and TDEE shows the last neck/waist (hip too for women) with when they were logged and a Log action. When the weekly check is due it gets a small amber dot and DUE, not a pop-up "Log now / Not now" nag. It stays on Today, undoing v1's move to History. The 40 / 95 cm figures are placeholders.
  • Workout gets its own compact row next to water and weight (Legs, 45 min, moderate, 486 kcal, with Log workout and paste log for pro).
  • Unchanged: header, mode lamps, tapes, water strip, weight and TDEE, the 7-day lamps, zero emoji, and the same six hand-drawn icons (History, Badges, Settings, photo, voice, text) on one 24px grid with a 1.75px stroke.
  • Signed-in chip. The Google profile chip is the last item in the header, after History, Badges and Settings. It is a 36px circle so it reads as a person, not a tool. It shows the photo, or the fallback shown here: the initial "D" (the dev-preview user) in the accent colour with a thin amber ring. It opens Account & Privacy. To fit at 390px the header buttons went from 38 to 36px with 5px gaps, and the date line is 11px.

Round 3 · Diet break (Option 1, chosen)

The cut-load tape

A thin instrument tape under the mode lamps, shown only while you're cutting or on a break. It fills as cut load builds, turns amber at the nudge point (56) and reads BREAK DUE when full (84). Tapping it opens the decision. A break is just Maintain: MAINTAIN is lit and the tape drains in teal over 14 days. The main gauge never changes.

From the spec
Copy, thresholds, "Remind me in 3 days" and "snoozable but never dismissable" all come from energy-safety/02 and 03. The tape shows weeks, not load units.
Decided
No BREAK label: a break is Maintain mode, as spec 03 already says. What happens if you go back to Cut early is being argued out by a swarm.

Diet break: cut-load tape

  • The tape is the spec’s bar, drawn as an instrument. It sits under the mode lamps and has the same tick scale as the macro tapes, with 12 ticks for 0–84 load. There is a taller mark at the nudge point (CUT_BLOCK_SOFT_NUDGE 56) and at the end (CUT_BLOCK_HARD_PROMPT 84). The label is always real elapsed weeks, never a load number (03: "its label shows my real elapsed weeks"). It shows only when there is something to show: while cutting from about a week in, or while load remains on Maintain or Bulk. A block a day old shows nothing (03, CUT_BAR_MIN_LOAD).
  • 1 · Mid-cut. 6 weeks at a 20% deficit is about 42 load (1.0 per day at REFERENCE_DEFICIT, 02). The fill and pointer are neutral grey, and the right label just says "6 weeks". Nothing asks for attention.
  • 2 · Break due. At 84 load (12 weeks at 20%; 02's examples show 24 weeks at 10%) the fill turns amber and reads BREAK DUE · 12 WEEKS. From 56 to 84 it is already amber, and tapping it opens 02's soft nudge: "You've been cutting for N weeks", "Start a 2-week break" or "Not yet", which hides it for 7 days. The week count keeps climbing while it waits (02: "four weeks later it reads 20 weeks").
  • 3 · Tapped. The hard prompt becomes a bottom sheet using 02's own copy: "Time for a diet break", "N weeks is a long stretch in a deficit. Let's spend a couple of weeks at maintenance." The buttons are "Start a 2-week break" and "Remind me in 3 days". There is no close or "never ask again", because the hard prompt is snoozable but never dismissable. 02 asks for a red-bordered card; here amber carries "needs attention" instead of red. Starting the break is one tap with no confirm, followed by the toast "Break started — eat at maintenance and recharge" (03).
  • 4 · Day 5 of break (Maintain). A break is simply Maintain mode, not a fourth mode (03: "my mode is Maintain, the same Maintain as the mode picker's chip"). So MAINTAIN is lit in its normal style and CUT is off. The tape stays under the lamps, in muted teal (#7cbfb4) so amber stays reserved for attention, and reads CUT LOAD · ON A BREAK · DAY 5 OF 14. It drains right to left by 1/14 per rest day (DIET_BREAK_DAYS): at 84 × 9/14 it is about 54 left, and the dashed outline is what has already drained. The gauge works exactly as before, only with the maintenance target: 2,741 − 1,150 = 1,591 still to go. Tapping the tape shows 03's lines: "about 9 days to fully recharged" and "Recharging now sets up your next block".
  • Settled and open. Settled: there is no BREAK lamp. The break shows as Maintain lit, plus the teal draining tape, which is the only sign it is a break. Open: with today Fri 20 Nov as day 5, full recharge lands about Sun 29 Nov, not Thu 3 Dec. The label says "day 5 of 14" and leaves the date to the tap-through, worded as an estimate as 03 requires.
Round 3 · Break days must be real

Only days eaten at maintenance count

A break day is banked only if you ate at least 90% of maintenance (2,470 of 2,741). Eating over is fine. A day under maintenance doesn't come off the counter and shows as a gap on the tape. Each day is judged once it ends, and an unlogged day stays pending until you log it.

Revised: W5 now ends "Eat at maintenance.", W6 is "Yesterday wasn't logged.", and W10 adds the run-of-days line "5 for 5. This is what a break looks like." All lines are numbered W1–W10 under the phones.

Round 3 · Over target

Over target: colour only on a cut

In CUT mode the gauge line is green up to the target, amber up to 250 over and red beyond that. At or over target the arc is simply full, with no extension past F, so only the colour shows how the day went. The number stays plain ink while you're on target, and the readout flips to "N over" once you pass it. Today always looks like a cut day. A day eaten at maintenance or above is saved to History as Maintain (or Bulk), and your chosen mode never changes. Maintain and Bulk modes are neutral, with no colour at all.

Data rule
The day type is stored separately from the chosen mode, so tomorrow still opens in Cut (the v99 bug class).
Knock-on
A maintenance-level day drains cut load like a banked break day.

Light theme

  • Daylight instrument, not paper. The stage is a cool blue-grey (#c5ced4), the phone ground slightly lighter (#e6ebee), and panels are near-white (#f8fafb). Groups are set apart by tone and a 1px soft elevation, not borders. No cream or beige.
  • Graphite ink. Text is #1e2529 (about 15:1 on panels). Quiet labels are #56626a, which is at least 4.3:1 on every surface, so the small mono caps still pass.
  • The gauge well is recessed. It uses a darker tone (#dce2e6) and a soft inset shadow. The scale ticks are a mid grey, and the unfilled track is a pale grey.
  • Amber is split in three. #ffb23e only as a fill, always with dark text (LOG FOOD, Save, the badge count; 10:1). #9a5a00 for amber text (5.2:1 on white, 4.6:1 on the ground; the 68px readout clears the large-text 3:1 even on the well). #b86e00 for marks: arc, pointer, tape pointers and the DUE dot (3:1 or more, as a graphic).
  • Signals that must stay clear. The DUE dot is drawn slightly larger (7px) in deep amber beside the amber-ink label. Delete is brick #b0412e, 5.5:1, still a text button with no fill. The lit lamp and the 7-day lamps are a pale amber tint with an amber-ink letter, and no glow.
  • Same layout, fonts and six icons as v2 dark. Inputs become white wells with a grey hairline, and the focused field gets a deep-amber edge with a pale amber halo ring (a 2px tint, not a glow).
  • Signed-in chip. The Google profile chip is the last item in the header, after History, Badges and Settings. It is a 36px circle so it reads as a person, not a tool. It shows the photo, or the fallback shown here: the initial "D" (the dev-preview user) in the accent colour with a thin deep-ochre (#b86e00) ring. It opens Account & Privacy. Round 1 below

The original diagnosis and the three directions, kept for reference.

Why it reads as generated

Ranked by how much each one contributes. Each item was checked against app.jsx, index.html and the screenshots; two suspects that didn't hold up were dropped.

  1. Emoji are doing the icons' job142 emoji, 62 kinds; 118 reach the screen. ⚡ has three meanings. "Done" is ✓, ✅ or 👍. "Premium" is ⭐, 🔐, 🔏 or 🔓. The badge "Top Recorder" is a flute 🪈. They render differently on every OS and ignore the theme colours.
    One line-icon set of 15 glyphs, one meaning each, coloured by the theme. Remove decorative emoji from copy. Keep emoji only on Achievements badges.
  2. Nine identical boxes, so nothing leadsThe same box style is pasted 26 times in app.jsx, with six different corner radii (10–20px). Every section has the same weight.
    Choose one hero. Group the rest by surface and spacing, and keep a box only for the one element that needs setting apart.

Design moves

  • One instrument, one number. The two competing figures (consumed vs remaining) and the blue progress bar become a fuel gauge with an E–F arc and a single amber readout: what's left. The arithmetic sits underneath as a sum, not as three more cards.
  • Amber avionics, not "success blue". One signal colour borrowed from cockpit displays, used only for live state: the readout, the needle, the active mode lamp, the primary action. No red/green verdicts anywhere.
  • Mode as annunciator lamps. CUT/MAINTAIN/BULK read like panel switches, one lit. The donut pair (Today/This week) is gone: the fat nudge becomes one line of copy under the macros, the week verdict becomes seven lit day lamps.
  • Macros as a single row of tapes. Three tick scales with a pointer, neutral fill, figures in JetBrains Mono tabular. "Not there yet" is just a position on a scale, not a red alarm.
  • The day's log is a printed fuel docket. A perforated paper slip with times in a left gutter, kcal right-aligned in a column, a NOW marker and a dashed total. It is the only light surface, so the ledger reads as the record, the panel as the instruments.
  • Workout logging moved into the docket as a pending line at NOW (Legs · 45 min · moderate · 486), with paste-log (pro) beside it, instead of a form card at the top of the screen. Body-weight copy shortened to one line; the measurements prompt moves to History.
  • Thumb-reach action bar. LOG FOOD (AI photo/voice/text) and QUICK ADD live in a persistent bottom bar instead of two equal tiles mid-scroll. History, Badges and Settings are line glyphs top-right; streak lives in the date line.
  • Zero emoji; icons only where words don't already say it. Every glyph is drawn on the same 24px grid with one 1.75px round-capped stroke and slightly wobbly hand-drawn lines, so the set reads as one hand. Only six exist: History, Badges, Settings (no room for labels) and photo / voice / text on LOG FOOD (they name the input methods). Mode, water, weight, macros and quick add are labelled, so they get no icon at all.
  • No card stack. Barlow Condensed for labels, Barlow for prose, JetBrains Mono for every number. Grouping comes from surface (gauge well, panel cells, paper) rather than identical rounded boxes; rules appear only where they mean something (a sum, a total, now).

Direction C: one job

The Today screen answers one question: how much can I still eat, and what have I eaten? Everything else is one tap away, not one scroll past.
